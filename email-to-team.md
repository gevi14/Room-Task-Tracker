# Email — Room Tracker Update Announcement

Paste the text below into your email client. Attach `Room-Tracker-Whats-New.pdf` (in your outputs folder) before sending. Fill in the bracketed parts.

---

**Subject:** Room Tracker — new features just rolled out

**To:** [your team distribution list]

---

Hi team,

I just rolled out a batch of updates to the Room Tracker app, and I wanted to walk you through what changed before you next open it. Full release notes with screenshots are attached, but here are the highlights.

**What's new:**

- **A new fifth task on every room: Closet Hooks.** It shows up as the last column on every floor of both towers.
- **East Tower Floor 1** is now in the system, with rooms 114, 117, and 118.
- **Color-coded percentages.** Each task at the top of the page is now red, orange, yellow, or green depending on how far along it is — so you can spot what's running behind at a glance.
- **"Select all" button on every task column.** Under each task name on the floor table, tap the small "Select all" pill and every room on the current floor gets marked done for that task. The button turns green and reads "Clear all" once the whole column is done, in case you need to undo it.
- **Click any task percentage** at the top of the page to open a detail view that lists every room with that task across both towers and every floor. Green pills = done, gray pills = not done. Tap any room pill to toggle its status.
- **Filter** the detail view by All, Completed, or Incomplete — useful for tracking down the last few stragglers at end of shift.
- **Dark / light theme toggle.** Sun/moon icon in the top-right of the header — pick whichever's easier on your eyes.

**The app is at:**

https://gevi14.github.io/Room-Task-Tracker/

Open it on your phone in **Safari** (not Chrome), tap the **Share** button at the bottom, then **"Add to Home Screen"** — it'll install like a real app and open with one tap from then on. When the welcome screen asks for your name, use your real first name so every check stamps who did it.

If anything looks off or you have ideas for the next update, hit reply or come find me.

Thanks,
[Your name]
