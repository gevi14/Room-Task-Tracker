## After-sending checklist for you

- These changes are pure UX improvements — no new features anyone has to learn, no behavior change. So an SMS heads-up is plenty; no full email needed.
- If anyone replies "I don't see a change" — they need to hard-refresh. On iPhone PWA: close from app switcher + reopen. In Safari/Chrome: Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+R.
- To verify they're on the new version, ask them to open the app and check that the floor table appears almost immediately (rather than scrolling past five big task cards first).
